The Sourcing Problem

Sourcing early-stage deals is harder than ever

A shrinking pipeline and network-closed rounds mean standard deal channels are drying up.

01

Active investors in Indian rounds collapsed 53%

Collapsing from ~6,800 to ~3,170 in a single year. The funds still deploying face the same shrinking, over-fished pipeline, making proprietary networks vital. (Tracxn, 2025)

02

The best deals never reach your inbox

Campus founders and Tier-2 city startups close rounds through networks before they ever hit an institutional pipeline. Associates can't be everywhere at once.

03

Deal count fell nearly 39% in 2025

Fewer, bigger, more contested deals (falling to 1,518 rounds), while total funding slipped 17% to $10.5B. Sourcing the right ones matters more than ever. (Tracxn, 2025)

04

Seed funding grew 18% YoY to $478M

Seed funding grew 18% YoY to $478M in H1 2026, with deal volume up 7%. Capital is rotating to the earliest stage, exactly where fund coverage is thinnest and network-driven sourcing wins. (Inc42, 2026)

Diligence Filter

The VRaise Filter

Deep Dive into how we run our "Due Diligence"

We don't just refer deals. We put founders through a rigorous vetting process that validates metrics, integrity, and momentum, giving partners institutional-grade confidence.

Monitoring

Every referral ships with an honest memo. Risks upfront, weaknesses included. If a startup fails our process, it never reaches your inbox.

Execution

Numbers recalculated from raw data. Inflated GMV, LOIs counted as customers, we catch it before a fund ever sees the deck.

Planning

We talk to real customers, not just the ones founders hand us. Past mentors, accelerator peers, and users who left tell us what the deck won't.

Prioritization

We test the founder, not the pitch. Real tasks, hard questions, tight deadlines. Speed and honesty tell us everything.

Assessment

We track founders months before they raise. Demo days, hackathons, founder groups. We see teams being built, not just pitching.
